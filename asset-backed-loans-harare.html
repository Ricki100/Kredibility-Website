Asset-Backed Loans in Harare: Borrowing Against Your Car or Equipment

How asset-backed loans in Harare work, what assets can be used, the documents needed and what happens to your asset while you repay.

Apply Now Talk to a consultant

By Kredibility Finance · Published 22 October 2026

If you own a vehicle, machinery or other durable goods, asset-backed loans in Harare let you use that asset as security. Because the asset reduces the lender's risk, this can unlock credit even when your payslip or business records are limited. This guide explains how it works.

What assets can be used?

Kredibility's asset-backed loans can be secured against:

  • Vehicles
  • Business equipment and machinery
  • Durable goods of sufficient value
  • Structured asset purchases

The asset needs to be in your name, with proof of ownership, and be of enough value to support the facility.

Documents you need

  • National ID
  • Proof of ownership, such as a vehicle logbook, purchase receipt or certificate
  • Basic income or revenue documentation
  • Bank account details

What happens to your asset?

You keep using your asset during the repayment period. It acts as security for the loan, and the agreement sets out the lender's rights if the loan is not repaid. Read this part of your agreement carefully, and ask your consultant to explain anything that is unclear.

How fast is a decision?

Asset-backed facilities usually require 48 to 72 hours from receiving complete documents, because the asset needs to be verified as well as your income.

Is an asset-backed loan right for you?

  • Yes, if you have a valuable asset and limited income paperwork, or you need a larger amount than a salary loan allows.
  • Consider alternatives if you have a steady payslip, where a private sector employee loans or civil servant payroll loans may be simpler.
  • Business owners with strong bank statements can also look at SME business loans without collateral. See business loans without collateral.

Before you sign

Your client loan schedule shows every instalment and everything payable. Interest is a fixed rate on a reducing balance, and loan insurance covers death, involuntary job loss and permanent disability. Rates, early settlement and missed payments are discussed with you when the facility is structured.

In short: asset-backed loans in Harare turn what you own into access to credit, while you keep using it. Find out more on our asset-backed loans page, or apply online.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a loan using my car as security in Harare?

Yes. Kredibility offers asset-backed loans secured against vehicles, equipment and other durable goods, with proof of ownership and basic income documentation.

Do I keep my car during an asset-backed loan?

Yes. You keep using the asset during the repayment period. It acts as security, and the agreement explains what happens if the loan is not repaid.

How long does an asset-backed loan take?

Usually 48 to 72 hours after Kredibility receives complete documents, because the asset is verified as well as your income.
