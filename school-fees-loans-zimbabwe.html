School Fees Loans in Zimbabwe: How to Spread the Cost of a Term

How school fees loans in Zimbabwe work, when to apply before term starts, the documents you need and how to choose a repayment you can manage.

Apply Now Talk to a consultant

By Kredibility Finance · Published 22 October 2026

School fees often arrive all at once, at the start of each term. School fees loans in Zimbabwe let you pay the school on time and spread the cost over a few months. This guide explains how education loans work, when to apply, and how to keep the repayments manageable.

What a school fees loan covers

Kredibility's school fees loans can cover education costs for primary, secondary and college or university learners, subject to eligibility, verification and an affordability assessment.

When to apply

Demand for school fees financing rises sharply in January, April and September, at the start of each term. Apply a few weeks early, so your loan is in place before the fees are due and you are not rushed into a decision.

Who can apply?

Anyone with verifiable income can enquire, whether you are employed or run a business. The loan is sized to what you can repay over a short, defined period.

Documents you need

  • National ID
  • Proof of income, such as a payslip, bank statements or business records
  • Bank account details
  • Information about the school fees you need to pay, such as an invoice or fee statement

Do not send a child's ID or personal details through the website form. A consultant will confirm exactly what is needed.

How quickly is it assessed?

Complete, qualifying applications may be assessed within 24 hours. Timing depends on receiving all the information and completing verification.

Keeping repayments manageable

  • Borrow only the fees you need for this term, not extras.
  • Aim to finish repaying before the next term's fees are due, so the loans do not stack up.
  • Check your total monthly commitments, including any salary deductions, before you apply.
  • Read your client loan schedule, which shows every instalment and everything payable.

Interest is a fixed rate on a reducing balance. For current rates, contact the credit team. Early settlement and missed payments are discussed with you when your facility is structured.

Other options

If you are a civil servant, a civil servant payroll loans with a stop order can also cover school fees. For other urgent costs, see emergency loans in Harare.

In short: school fees loans in Zimbabwe keep your child in class while you spread the cost. Apply early in the term cycle, and start your enquiry on our school fees loans page.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kredibility offer education loans in Zimbabwe?

Yes. Kredibility's school fees financing covers primary, secondary and college or university learners, subject to eligibility, verification and affordability.

When should I apply for a school fees loan?

A few weeks before term starts. Applications usually rise in January, April and September.

How quickly are school fees loans assessed?

Complete, qualifying applications may be assessed within 24 hours, depending on verification.
