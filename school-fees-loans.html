Before you enquire

Term 3 school fees questions, answered.

Who can apply for a school fees loan in Zimbabwe?

People with verifiable income may enquire. Approval remains subject to Kredibility’s eligibility, verification, credit and affordability assessment.

What documents are required?

A complete application generally requires identification, proof of income, bank details, the completed application form and supporting information about the school-fees need. A consultant confirms the exact requirements.

How quickly are school fees loans assessed?

Complete qualifying applications may be assessed within 24 hours. Timing depends on receiving all required information and completing verification.

Does submitting this form guarantee approval?

No. The form starts an enquiry. Approval and final terms depend on eligibility, verification, affordability and Kredibility’s credit assessment.
