Before you enquire

SME loan questions, answered.

What is an SME business loan?

An SME business loan provides working capital or business finance for eligible owners, subject to verification, credit and affordability assessment.

Who can enquire about a Kredibility SME loan?

SME owners and self-employed business operators with verifiable trading activity may enquire. Approval is not guaranteed and remains subject to eligibility, verification and affordability assessment.

Does submitting the SME loan form guarantee approval?

No. The form starts an enquiry. A consultant explains the required documents, assessment process, applicable pricing and repayment terms before any facility is offered.

What can an SME loan be used for?

Business finance may support stock, equipment, working capital or business expenses, depending on your circumstances and the assessment outcome.
