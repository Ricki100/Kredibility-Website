Before you enquire

SSB smartphone-loan questions.

Who is this smartphone offer for?

This campaign is exclusively for Zimbabwean employees whose salaries are processed through the Salary Service Bureau (SSB). Eligibility and applicable terms are confirmed during the enquiry.

Is this the full Kredibility smartphone catalogue?

No. These five models are a selected preview. Eligible SSB employees can complete the form to request the full current catalogue on WhatsApp.

Are the prices and availability guaranteed?

No. Prices and availability can change. A consultant confirms the current catalogue, stock and applicable terms when you enquire.

How do I request the full catalogue?

Complete the short enquiry form. We record your contact details and open WhatsApp with a prepared catalogue request.
