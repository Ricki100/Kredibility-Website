Before you enquire

ZiG loan questions, answered.

What is a ZiG loan?

A ZiG loan is credit denominated in Zimbabwe Gold, Zimbabwe’s local currency. Kredibility assesses each enquiry against eligibility, verifiable income and affordability requirements.

Who can enquire about a Kredibility ZiG loan?

Formally employed people and SME owners with verifiable income may enquire. Approval is not guaranteed and remains subject to eligibility, verification and affordability assessment.

Does submitting the ZiG loan form guarantee approval?

No. The form starts an enquiry. A consultant explains the required documents, assessment process, applicable pricing and repayment terms before any facility is offered.

Can I use a ZiG loan for personal or business needs?

Kredibility considers enquiries from income-earning individuals and SME owners. The available facility and permitted purpose depend on your circumstances and the assessment outcome.
