Chief Executive Officer

Success Tatenda Hlanganiso

Success Tatenda Hlanganiso brings over 8 years of experience in credit operations and microfinance leadership. He is a seasoned finance professional with proven expertise in credit risk management, business development, operational strategy, and portfolio growth within Zimbabwe’s financial services sector.

Throughout his career, Success has held senior leadership positions in the microfinance industry, where he successfully led regional expansion initiatives, strengthened operational systems, improved portfolio performance, and drove sustainable business growth. His experience spans SME finance, lending operations, financial management, and strategic leadership.

Governance Principles

Integrity, discipline, accountability.

The leadership model at Kredibility Finance is built on three principles: integrity in every client relationship, discipline in every credit decision, and accountability to borrowers, regulators and the institution itself.

These principles are not aspirational statements — they are embedded in the governance framework, risk management processes and daily operations of the institution.

Institutional Direction

Championing growth and financial inclusion.

At Kredibility Finance, Success is championing the institution’s growth and strategic development with a strong focus on financial inclusion, innovation, operational excellence, and customer-oriented financial solutions — building a diversified, resilient loan book that serves Zimbabwe’s employees and businesses.
