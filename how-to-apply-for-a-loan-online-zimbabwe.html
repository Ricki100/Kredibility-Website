How to Apply for a Loan Online in Zimbabwe: Documents, Steps and Approval Times

How to apply for a loan online in Zimbabwe: the documents you need, each step of the process, how long approval takes and what to check before you sign.

Apply Now Talk to a consultant

By Kredibility Finance · Published 22 October 2026

More Zimbabweans now apply for a loan online, rather than queuing at a branch. The process is faster, but the basics have not changed: you still need the right documents, an affordability check and a written agreement. This guide walks through each step with Kredibility Finance.

Step 1: Choose the right type of loan

Your choice depends on how you earn. Civil servants usually choose a payroll loan, formally employed people a private sector loan, and business owners an SME loan. Compare all Kredibility loan products, or read our overview of types of loans in Harare.

Step 2: Complete the online application

Go to the apply online page and fill in your contact details, employer or business, the amount you need and your preferred repayment period. There is no application fee. You will receive an acknowledgement within 2 hours of submitting.

Step 3: Prepare your documents

Loan typeDocuments usually needed
Civil servant payroll loanNational ID, current payslip, employer confirmation letter
Private sector loanNational ID, payslips (last 3 months preferred), employer confirmation letter, bank statements (last 3 months)
SME business loanNational ID, business registration or trading evidence, bank statements (last 3–6 months)
Asset-backed loanNational ID, proof of ownership (logbook, receipt or certificate), basic income or revenue records
School fees or emergency loanNational ID, proof of income (payslip, business statement or equivalent)

Every applicant also needs bank account details, because approved loans are paid only into the account named in the application. The credit team will tell you exactly what is required, and how to send it securely.

Step 4: Verification and affordability assessment

Kredibility confirms your identity, income and documents, and may check your credit record. The aim is to make sure the repayments fit comfortably within your income. If you do not qualify for the full amount you asked for, you may be offered a smaller amount that you can afford.

Step 5: How long does approval take?

  • Payroll (civil servant) loans: typically within 24 hours of receiving complete documents.
  • Private sector loans: typically 24 to 48 hours.
  • SME and asset-backed loans: usually 48 to 72 hours.

Incomplete documents are the most common cause of delay, so send everything the credit team asks for in one go.

Step 6: Read the terms before you sign

If your application is approved, you receive a client loan schedule showing your instalments and everything payable. Interest is a fixed rate on a reducing balance, and establishment fees are either paid upfront or deducted before the loan is paid out. Rates, early settlement and what happens if a payment is missed are discussed with you when your facility is structured. Read our Terms & Conditions in full.

After the money reaches your account, you have a three-working-day cooling-off period.

Tips for a smooth online application

  • Use the same name and details as on your national ID.
  • Make sure your payslip or bank statements are recent and clear.
  • Only borrow what you can repay comfortably each month.
  • Never share your ID or bank details on social media or with unverified callers. Kredibility will only collect documents through its own credit team.

In short: to apply for a loan online in Zimbabwe, choose the right product, prepare your documents, and read every term before you sign. When you are ready, apply online.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a Kredibility loan online?

Yes. Complete the online application form and the credit team will contact you to verify your details and collect supporting documents.

What documents do I need for a loan in Zimbabwe?

Usually a national ID, proof of income (a payslip, bank statements or business records), employer or business confirmation, and bank account details. The exact list depends on the loan type.

Is there an application fee?

There is no application fee to apply with Kredibility. Any establishment fees on an approved loan are shown in your client loan schedule before you sign.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Every application is subject to verification, a credit check and an affordability assessment.
