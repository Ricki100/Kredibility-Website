Cookie Policy

How Kredibility Finance uses essential storage and optional analytics and advertising technologies.

Effective date: 8 August 2026

This policy explains the browser storage and third-party technologies used on this site. Non-essential analytics and advertising technologies remain blocked until you make the corresponding choice.

1. Your choices

You may accept all, reject non-essential technologies or choose categories separately. Rejecting them does not block access to the website or loan form. You can change your choice at any time.

2. Technology inventory

TechnologyCategoryPurposeTypical storage
kredibility_privacy_preferencesEssentialStores your category choices and consent version in local browser storage.Until cleared or the consent version changes
Google Analytics (_ga and related identifiers)AnalyticsAggregated website usage, traffic sources and consented events. IP anonymisation is requested in configuration.Provider-controlled; commonly up to 2 years
Microsoft Clarity (_clck, _clsk and related identifiers)AnalyticsConsented interaction and usability analysis. Clarity is disabled on the application page.Provider-controlled; commonly 1 day to 1 year
Meta Pixel (_fbp, _fbc where applicable)AdvertisingCampaign measurement, attribution and remarketing after advertising consent.Provider-controlled; commonly up to 90 days

3. Information received by providers

Depending on the chosen category, providers may receive IP address, browser/device details, page URL, referral information, timestamps, identifiers and interactions. Kredibility does not deliberately place national ID, income, account or application-field values in analytics or advertising events.

4. Withdrawal

Changing your choice prevents future loading through this consent manager. It cannot automatically erase information already received by a provider. You may also clear browser storage and use provider or browser privacy controls.

5. Contact

Questions may be sent to admin@kredibilityfinance.co.zw. See the Privacy Policy for rights, recipients and international-processing information.
