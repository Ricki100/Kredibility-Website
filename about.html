Executive Overview

Built for formal and semi-formal income segments

Kredibility Finance delivers structured, transparent and accessible financial services to Zimbabwe’s formally employed workforce and economically active population. We operate within the Microfinance Act framework and adhere to the directives of the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe, ZIMRA and the Public Service Commission.

Vision

Trusted and diversified

To become Zimbabwe’s most trusted and diversified microfinance institution, recognised for integrity, regulatory discipline and responsible credit management. Not a narrow payroll-only lender — a disciplined financial institution capable of assessing and structuring facilities across multiple income types.

Mission

Structured, transparent, affordable

To provide structured, transparent and affordable micro-credit solutions that enhance financial resilience, support asset acquisition and promote sustainable growth among Zimbabwe’s formally employed and economically active population.

Strategic Intent

Portfolio balance. Disciplined diversification.

Civil servants provide a strong institutional foundation through payroll-backed stability. Our expansion strategy goes further — reaching private sector employees, SMEs, asset-backed borrowers and commission-based professionals to build a balanced, resilient loan portfolio.
