Business Loans in Zimbabwe Without Collateral: What SMEs Can Actually Get

Can you get business loans in Zimbabwe without collateral? How SME loans are assessed on cash flow, the documents needed and how to strengthen your case.

Apply Now Talk to a consultant

By Kredibility Finance · Published 22 October 2026

Many small business owners assume they need a house or a vehicle as security before anyone will lend to them. That is not always true. Business loans in Zimbabwe without collateral are possible when a lender assesses your business on its cash flow. This guide explains how that works, and how to give your application the best chance.

How cash-flow lending works

Instead of relying on an asset, the lender looks at money moving through your business: deposits, sales and regular expenses. If your revenue can comfortably cover the repayments, the loan can be structured around your trading cycle.

Kredibility's SME business loans are assessed this way. You do not need a payslip, and loans are available in US dollars or ZiG.

Who qualifies?

  • Traders, shop owners, contractors and growing enterprises operating in Zimbabwe.
  • A business that is economically active, with verifiable revenue.
  • A valid national ID for the owner.

Documents you need

  • National ID
  • Business registration or other evidence of trading
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months

If your business is informal or newly registered, trading records and documented revenue can still be used. Ask the credit team what will work for you.

How fast is a decision?

SME facilities are usually decided within 48 to 72 hours of receiving complete documents.

How to strengthen your application

  1. Run your sales through one business bank account, so your revenue is visible.
  2. Keep simple records of sales, stock and expenses, even in a notebook or spreadsheet.
  3. Know exactly what the money is for, such as stock, equipment or a contract, and how it will pay for itself.
  4. Ask for an amount your monthly cash flow can repay with room to spare.
  5. Separate personal and business spending wherever you can.

What about start-ups?

Lenders need evidence that a business can repay, so a brand-new business with no trading history is harder to finance on cash flow alone. If you are just starting out, building a few months of bank statements first, or considering an asset-backed facility, can help.

If you own a vehicle or equipment, an asset-backed loans may unlock a larger amount. See asset-backed loans in Harare.

Before you sign

Your client loan schedule sets out every instalment and everything payable. Interest is a fixed rate on a reducing balance. Pricing, early settlement and what happens if a payment is missed are discussed with your consultant when the facility is structured.

In short: business loans in Zimbabwe without collateral are realistic when you can show steady revenue. Get your statements in order, then explore our SME business loans or apply online.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a business loan in Zimbabwe without collateral?

Yes, if your business has verifiable cash flow. Kredibility assesses SME loans on business bank statements, trading records or documented revenue rather than collateral or a payslip.

Are business loans available in USD or ZiG?

Kredibility's SME loans are available in US dollars or Zimbabwe Gold (ZiG), depending on your business needs and assessment.

How long does an SME loan decision take?

SME facilities are usually decided within 48 to 72 hours of receiving complete documentation.

Can a new business get a loan?

It is harder without trading history. Building a few months of bank statements, or using an asset as security, improves your chances.
