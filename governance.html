Risk Appetite

Conservative by design

Kredibility operates under a structured and conservative risk appetite framework that supports diversified portfolio growth while maintaining capital preservation. Every facility is assessed, documented and priced within this framework.

Regulatory Alignment

RBZ, ZIMRA & Microfinance Act

The institution operates within Zimbabwe’s statutory microfinance framework. Compliance with the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe, ZIMRA and the Public Service Commission is not optional — it is the foundation of every decision.

Portfolio Strategy

Diversification with discipline

The institution deliberately avoids over-concentration in any single market segment. Portfolio segmentation reduces systemic exposure and enhances long-term institutional resilience without compromising underwriting standards.

Six Pillars

Risk controls across every facility.

These six pillars govern every credit decision Kredibility makes. No exceptions.

01

Payroll Stability

Payroll-backed lending provides a stabilising core. Centralised deduction mechanisms reduce default risk substantially.

02

Income Verification

All non-payroll clients undergo income verification. Documentation is required before any facility is structured.

03

Affordability Assessment

Every applicant undergoes an affordability assessment. Facilities are sized to what borrowers can genuinely afford to repay.

04

Asset Security

Asset-backed facilities require verified collateral. Security structures are documented and disclosed to borrowers upfront.

05

Internal Audit Oversight

Internal audit processes monitor portfolio quality, documentation standards and regulatory compliance on an ongoing basis.

06

Fraud Detection

Active fraud detection systems protect both the institution and borrowers from identity fraud and misrepresentation.
